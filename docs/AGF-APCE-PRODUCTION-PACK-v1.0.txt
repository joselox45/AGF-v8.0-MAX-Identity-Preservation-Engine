    Mutation rejected or surfaced according to the accepted invariant.

Expected:
    Higher-priority controls remain active.

Acceptance:
    No authority inversion.

T09 — Rollback
Input:
    Activation is declared invalid or a critical test fails.

Expected:
    AGF candidate controls are deactivated.

Acceptance:

T10 — Regression

Expected:

Acceptance:

15. ROLLBACK CONTRACT
================================================================================

Trigger rollback when:

    a material conflict is discovered;
    an activated control violates APCE precedence;
    a test that is required for activation fails;
    a critical postcondition cannot be verified;
    source lineage becomes uncertain;
    unauthorized scope escalation is detected;
    identity/invariant behavior becomes ambiguous;
    the runtime cannot distinguish AGF behavior from APCE behavior.

Rollback action:

    1. Disable AGF candidate controls.
    2. Preserve APCE canonical baseline.
    3. Record failure and evidence.
    4. Mark affected assurance state FAILED/BLOCKED.
    5. Do not claim successful activation.
